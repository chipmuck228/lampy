## 中文

个人记录与个人生活册默认保存在本机，未主动分享的内容不上传到Lampy家庭服务。主动分享时，所选文字、感受、日期、照片与声音副本，以及家庭册的名称、开篇和整理信息，会保存在家庭服务，供有资格的家庭成员使用。

Apple登录用于建立家庭身份；正式登录不请求姓名或邮箱。服务保存Apple账号标识、Lampy账号标识、昵称、会话和家庭成员关系。成员、角色、邀请与屏蔽关系仅用于家庭访问、管理和阅读过滤，不读取手机通讯录。

为防止登录滥用，服务保存来源IP、限流窗口时间和次数，不用于定位、广告分析或跨服务追踪，也不为这些目的对外提供。云服务提供商代表Lampy处理服务器数据。限流窗口不是删除期限，当前版本尚未为全部旧限流记录设置统一的自动到期删除规则。

举报保存原因、补充说明、相关账号与分享引用、状态及处理记录。运营人员可为处理举报核对被举报的家庭内容。举报不会另外复制记录正文或媒体；相关内容仍按家庭分享的访问规则处理。举报与处理记录没有统一的按天到期规则，相关账号和家庭删除规则仍适用。

撤回、账号删除和家庭解散会按应用说明限制访问并执行相关清理，媒体删除失败会排队重试。本机私人记录不因账号删除而删除。运维备份、系统日志和云快照独立保留并按各自配置到期处理，不承诺立即物理擦除所有副本；恢复需核对撤回、删除和下架状态。Lampy不提供私人记录云备份或异常恢复服务。支持与隐私咨询：dev@yunpura.com。

## English

Personal records and personal albums stay on your device by default. Unshared content is not uploaded to Lampy's family service. When you choose to share, selected text, feelings, dates, photo and audio copies, and family album names, openings and organization data are stored by the family service for eligible family members.

Apple sign-in establishes a family identity and does not request your name or email in the production flow. The service stores Apple and Lampy account identifiers, nicknames, sessions and family relationships. Membership, roles, invitations and blocks serve only family access, management and reading filters; Lampy does not read your phone address book.

To prevent login abuse, the service stores source IP addresses, rate-limit window times and counts. These are not used or disclosed for location inference, advertising analytics or cross-service tracking. Cloud providers process server data on Lampy's behalf. A rate-limit window is not a deletion deadline; this version has no uniform automatic expiry for all old rate-limit records.

Reports retain reasons, additional explanations, related account and share references, status and moderation records. Operators may inspect reported family content to handle reports. Reports do not create separate copies of record text or media; that content remains subject to family-share access controls. Reports and moderation records have no uniform age-based expiry; applicable account and family deletion rules still apply.

Withdrawal, account deletion and family dissolution restrict access and trigger the applicable cleanup described in the app; failed media deletion is retried. Deleting an account does not delete private on-device records. Operational backups, system logs and cloud snapshots are retained separately and expire under their respective configurations. Immediate physical erasure of every copy is not promised; restoration requires reconciliation of withdrawn, deleted and moderated content. Lampy does not provide private-record cloud backup or exceptional recovery. Support and privacy inquiries: dev@yunpura.com.
