## 中文元数据候选

- 名称：Lampy
- 副标题：留下生活，慢慢回看
- 推广文本：留下一段话、一张照片，或一分钟的声音。独自回看，也可以把选定的生活片段分享给家人，整理成生活册。
- 关键词：生活记录,日记,声音,照片,家庭,回忆,生活册,时间,记录

描述：

生活里有些片刻，你只是想留下。

用文字、照片和声音记录日常。新录音每段最多一分钟，个人记录无需登录，默认保存在这台设备。

在「最近」看看刚留下的生活，在「回看」按时间阅读。打开日历，有记录的日期显示条数，点一天，读那一天的记录。

将记录收进生活册，选择封面、写下开篇、调整顺序。连续阅读，或切换素纸与手作，看看纸页里的样子。

想与家人一起记录时，通过 Apple 登录，创建家庭或接受邀请。只有主动分享的内容发送到家庭服务，供家人阅读、整理成家庭生活册。你可以撤回自己的分享，也可以举报或屏蔽不想看到的家庭内容。

本机保护可在离开应用时遮挡内容，返回时使用系统认证解锁。

Lampy 1.0.0 不提供购买、PDF／内容包导出或打印。Lampy 不提供私人记录云备份或异常恢复，请自行保管本机记录。删除家庭账号不会删除本机私人记录。

Description:

Some moments are simply worth keeping.

Capture everyday life in words, photos and sound. Each new recording can last up to one minute. Personal records do not require an account and stay on your device by default.

Read recent moments or revisit them by date. Open the calendar to see how many records each day holds, then choose a day to read.

Gather records into life albums. Choose a cover, write an opening and arrange the order. Read continuously or preview paper pages in the plain-paper and handmade styles.

For family sharing, sign in with Apple and create a family or accept an invitation. Only content you choose to share is sent to the family service. Family members can read it, and the creator can organize it into family albums. You can withdraw your shares, report content or block a family member's content from your view.

Optional device protection covers content when you leave the app and uses system authentication when you return.

Version 1.0.0 does not offer purchases, PDF or content-package export, or printing. Lampy does not provide personal-record cloud backup or exceptional data recovery. Keep your device records safe. Deleting a family account leaves personal records on your device.

## Review notes candidate

Lampy is a personal and private-family life recording app. Personal text, photos, audio and albums work without an account. New recordings are limited to one minute in version 1.0.0. Purchases, restore purchases, all personal/family exports and print evaluation are disabled.

Family features use Sign in with Apple. Sign-in does not automatically join a family: joining an invitation requires explicit confirmation. Account deletion is in Settings → Profile → Delete account and requires fresh Apple authorization. A creator with other active members must transfer or dissolve that family first. Deletion removes the cloud identity and the user's shares; local private records remain.

To report or block, open another family member's record and choose its overflow menu. Settings → Blocks and reports shows receipts and per-person unblock controls. The developer checks reports daily through a protected operator tool. Support and appeals: dev@yunpura.com. A sharing restriction prevents new shares; releasing it does not restore removed shares. Text filtering is limited; images and audio can be reported for human review.
