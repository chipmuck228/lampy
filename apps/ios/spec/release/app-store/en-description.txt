Some everyday moments are worth coming back to.

A few words after dinner. Rain outside the window. A familiar laugh. Lampy helps you keep life through text, photos and sound, then gather those moments into albums of your own.

KEEP THE MOMENT
Write a note, choose photos, or record the sound around you. New recordings can be up to one minute each.

REVISIT YOUR DAYS
Read your recent moments or open the calendar to see which days hold records and how many. Choose a date and return to what you left that day.

MAKE A LIFE ALBUM
Choose records and arrange their order. Give your album a name, a cover and an opening. Read it continuously or preview its pages in plain-paper and handmade styles.

SHARE A LITTLE WITH FAMILY
Use Sign in with Apple to create or join a family. Choose the text, photos and audio you want to share. Family members can read available shares, and creators can organize family life albums.

KEEP YOUR CHOICES
Private records do not require an account and stay on your device by default. Unshared content is not automatically uploaded to the family service. Optional App Lock uses system Face ID or your device passcode. You can withdraw family shares, report inappropriate content and hide a member’s shared content.

PLEASE NOTE
• Family features require Sign in with Apple and an internet connection.
• Keep your on-device records safe. Lampy does not provide cloud backup or a recovery service for private records.
• Version 1.0.0 does not offer purchases, PDF/content-package export or printing.
