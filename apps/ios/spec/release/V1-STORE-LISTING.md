# Lampy 1.0.0 — public store listing draft

Prepared 2026-10-26. Proposed public-facing copy and screenshot storyboard; no App Store Connect fields have been submitted. Copy-ready localized fields are in app-store/zh-Hans.json and app-store/en.json. Proposed names are subject to App Store Connect availability.

## Recommended listing

| Field | Simplified Chinese | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Name | Lampy · 生活记录与家庭生活册 | Lampy — Everyday Journal |
| Subtitle | 用文字、照片和声音，留下日常 | Photos, voice & family albums |
| Primary category | 生活 | Lifestyle |
| Optional secondary category | 工具 | Utilities |
| Keywords | 日记,回忆,相册,语音,录音,日历,随记,私人,亲子,手账 | diary,memories,notes,audio,recording,private,life,moments,keepsake,calendar,reflection |

Names and subtitles fit 30 characters, promotional text fits 170, and descriptions fit 4000. Keywords fit the stricter 100-byte interpretation: Chinese 69 UTF-8 bytes and English 86 bytes. Copyright must use the actual rights-holder name and year; do not paste an invented company or a placeholder. Category is a recommendation.

## Screenshot storyboard

| Order | Chinese headline | English headline | Actual app content |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 记录生活，原本的样子 | Keep life as it is | Recent records showing self-created notes and authorized everyday photos. |
| 2 | 把当时的声音，也留下 | Keep the sound of a moment | A saved short audio record/player or a recording draft, with real controls and a one-minute new recording limit. |
| 3 | 按日历，找回某一天 | Come back to a day | Actual Revisit calendar popup showing numeric record counts and real demo dates. |
| 4 | 慢慢整理成一本生活册 | Gather moments into an album | Personal album continuous reading, with title, opening and text/photo content. |
| 5 | 选一种喜欢的纸页 | Find your kind of page | Actual plain-paper and handmade page previews of the same album; no implied print/export action. |
| 6 | 把愿意分享的，留给家人 | Share a little with family | Family records or a family album using consented demonstration content; selected sharing, not automatic private-library synchronization. |

Use warm ivory #F6F0E3 or app paper #F3F0E9 with muted pine #40564A. One short headline per image, generous margins and real app UI occupying about 70–80% of the composition. Avoid imaginary controls, ratings, awards, unshipped features or poster-only images. Use the corresponding Chinese/English UI, not only translated captions. Do not expose personal family information or unconsented photos/voices.

Capture at accepted native screenshot sizes. The app supports iPhone and iPad, so prepare iPad assets as well rather than stretching iPhone images. Current Apple examples: iPhone medium portrait 1206×2622 or 1179×2556; iPad 13-inch portrait 2064×2752 or 2048×2732. Confirm the actual slots and resolutions in App Store Connect. A preview video is optional for the initial listing; prioritize six coherent still screenshots per selected device class and language.
